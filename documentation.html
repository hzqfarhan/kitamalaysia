Merdeka WebForge 2026 Submission

Kita Malaysia

An Interactive 3D Malaysian Cultural & Heritage Explorer

Project Executive Summary

Kita Malaysia is a modern, high-performance web experience crafted to celebrate Malaysia's rich tapestry of cultures, heritage, and national unity. Built with Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript, and Three.js, it unites Peninsular Malaysia and Malaysian Borneo through an interactive 3D miniature landscape, authentic cultural stories, an educational trivia quiz, and a gamified explorer passport. The application emphasizes accessibility, digital sovereignty (zero backend/API dependence), and deep reverence for Malaysia's living heritage.

01. Merdeka Theme & National Concept "Made of Many. Moving as One."

The Vision: Independence Through Mutual Respect & Unity

Independence (Merdeka) is more than a historical milestone achieved on August 31, 1957; it is a living commitment to mutual harmony, mutual understanding, and collective resilience. True national identity is forged not by erasing differences, but by celebrating how our diverse traditions, geography, and communities strengthen one another.

Kita Malaysia embodies this ethos by bringing Malaysian history and living traditions to the fingertips of the youth. Rather than presenting static textbook paragraphs, the website translates national heritage into an explorative, interactive spatial canvas.

Six Cultural Pillars Across East & West Malaysia

1. Kuala Lumpur Innovation

Rooted in culture, reaching for the sky. Showcases the PETRONAS Twin Towers, highlighting how modern architecture is grounded in Islamic geometric design (the eight-pointed star).

2. George Town (Penang) UNESCO Heritage

Streets of living exchange. Celebrates centuries of multicultural harmony, historic shophouses, and shared community spaces of the Straits of Malacca.

3. Melaka Living Heritage

The historic maritime crossroads. Traces the historical meeting of Malay, Portuguese, Dutch, and Peranakan cultures that laid the foundation of Malaysian pluralism.

4. Kelantan Art & Craft

Craftsmanship taking flight. Spotlights the traditional Wau Bulan kite, symbolizing patience, craftsmanship, and soaring national ambition.

5. Mount Kinabalu (Sabah) Nature & Ecology

A sacred ecosystem. Highlights Sabah's UNESCO World Heritage national park, its sacred cultural reverence by indigenous communities, and remarkable biodiversity.

6. Sarawak Community Unity

"Different doors. A shared space." Explores the philosophy of the indigenous longhouse — a profound architectural metaphor for national unity and harmony.

Educational Authenticity: All stories, historical milestones, and cultural facts are cross-referenced with accredited sources including the UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Tourism Malaysia, and the Sarawak Tourism Board.

02. Technical Architecture & Innovation Next.js 16 + Three.js

Core Technical Features

  • Interactive Three.js 3D Miniature Landscape: A low-poly illustrative 3D canvas featuring procedural terrain, customizable orbital camera controls (drag-to-orbit, scroll-to-zoom, auto-reset), animated cloud layers, and a flying Wau Bulan kite rendered in real-time WebGL.
  • Interactive Landmark Raycasting: Users interact with custom 3D pins representing historical regions, triggering synchronized story dialogs and geographic focus.
  • Gamified Explorer Passport: Six digital collectible passport stamps earned as visitors explore each state's story. Progress and high scores are preserved across browser sessions via localStorage with zero account barrier.
  • Malaysia Trivia Quiz Engine: A 5-question trivia experience testing insights gained across the explorer, featuring explanatory feedback and persistent high score tracking.
  • Accessibility First Design: Built-in skip links, native accessible dialogs (<dialog>), high-contrast typography, and prefers-reduced-motion overrides.

Performance & Production Optimization

The entire application compiles to a purely static bundle in out/. By utilizing procedural Three.js geometries for the landscape and optimizing photography, the application achieves lightning-fast first contentful paint (FCP), zero server-side latency, and 100% offline-ready reliability.
